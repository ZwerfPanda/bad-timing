Bad Timing Publisher

That's just bad timing.

Bad Timing Publisher is a private desktop tool. The owner of the Bad Timing YouTube channel uses it to upload the channel's own animated history videos and to schedule and update them. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy · Terms of service

Privacy policy

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

What it accesses. With your permission, the tool can upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the connected channel, and can read and update the title, description, tags and schedule of those videos.

Where the data stays. The sign-in tokens from Google are stored only on the computer that runs the tool. There is no server, no user account, no advertising and no tracking, and this website uses no cookies.

Sharing. Google user data is not sold and is not shared with anyone. It is sent only to YouTube, to do what you ask.

Removing access. You can withdraw the tool's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. Deleting the tool's token file removes the tokens from the computer.

Contact. Questions: badtimingvids@gmail.com.
